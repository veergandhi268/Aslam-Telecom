For over 15 years, Aslam Telecom has been Borivali East's most trusted sanctuary for damaged, dead, and rejected smartphones, MacBooks, and tablets.

Our Journey

From a Modest Counter to Borivali’s Premier Device Care Center

Founded by Master Technician Aslam in Shop No. 15 of Borivali's iconic Municipal Market, Aslam Telecom began with a singular principle: treat every customer’s device like an essential lifeline, not just another circuit board.

While authorized brand service centers routinely push customers to replace expensive motherboards when confronted with liquid ingress or sudden boot failures, our workshop invests heavily in precision micro-soldering tooling and schematics. We repair down to the individual resistor, diode, and integrated circuit.
